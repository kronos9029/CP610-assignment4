BEST K SELECTED: 2
================================================================================

Why K=2 was chosen:
  • Highest Silhouette Score: 0.3965
    (Silhouette measures cluster quality: -1 to 1, higher is better)
  • Davies-Bouldin Index: 0.9733
    (Davies-Bouldin measures separation: lower is better)
  • K=2 provides optimal balance of cluster cohesion and separation
